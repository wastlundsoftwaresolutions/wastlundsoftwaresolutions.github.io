Your account and data

An account is optional. Without one, your workouts stay only on your device.

  • Delete your account in the app (iOS or Android): open Settings, then Account, and choose Delete Account. After you confirm your password, the app permanently deletes your account, your email address and all workout data synced to it, and removes that data from the device.
  • Can't use the app? Email operation.vitality@gmail.com from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will delete the account and all workout data synced to it.
  • Remove data from one device only: in Settings, Account, choose Sign Out, then "Sign Out and Remove Data from This Device". Your account and its synced data stay.

More details are in the privacy policy.
